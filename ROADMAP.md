# Roadmap

The roadmap is deliberately limited to maintenance directions supported by the
current service boundary:

- expand checked-in policy fixtures for documented allow, explicit-deny, and
  deny-by-default cases;
- define explicit compatibility and versioning rules for the request, response,
  and policy formats;
- add operator-focused examples for local audit output and audit failures;
- strengthen boundary tests for upstream inputs, policy loading, downstream
  enforcement responsibility, and configured audit failure behavior.

These are directions, not committed release dates. Distributed policy
management and identity infrastructure remain outside the current scope.
